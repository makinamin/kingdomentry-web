Why now

The rules changed.

In 2025 Saudi Arabia replaced foreign investment licensing with a simpler registration system and introduced one national Commercial Registration. Equal treatment for foreign and local investors is now the law. Doors that were heavy are now open.

The question is no longer if you enter Saudi Arabia. It is how well.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A Kingdom Readiness Call and market scan. We test your product, sector and ambition against Saudi demand and regulation. You get a clear go, adjust or wait.
