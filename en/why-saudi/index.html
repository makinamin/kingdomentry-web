Vision 2030

Saudi Arabia is the largest economy in the Middle East and the fastest growing economy in the G20, according to the Ministry of Investment (MISA). Vision 2030 is diversifying the economy away from oil, toward technology, health, tourism, industry and clean energy. For international companies, this is a once-in-a-generation window.

Five reasons

Five reasons the Kingdom belongs on your map

  1. 01

    Fastest growing G20 economy.

    A young, digital and fast-urbanising population. Strong purchasing power. Massive public and private investment.

  2. 02

    Vision 2030.

    A national transformation program with clear targets, dedicated funding and giga-projects such as NEOM, the Red Sea and Qiddiya creating demand across every sector.

  3. 03

    Global market access.

    Positioned between Europe, Asia and Africa. A logistics and trade hub with direct reach into the GCC and wider MENA.

  4. 04

    Streamlined regulation, digital government services and investor-friendly policies.

  5. 05

    Government contract opportunities.

    Large-scale infrastructure, health, energy and digital projects open to qualified international suppliers.

New rules

The rules changed in your favour

February 2025

New Investment Law

Foreign investment licensing has been replaced by registration with MISA. Foreign and local investors are treated equally. A limited list of excluded activities remains, published in MISA's investor guide.

April 2025

Unified Commercial Registration

One national, non-expiring Commercial Registration replaces separate regional registrations.

December 2022

Apostille Convention

Saudi Arabia accepts apostilled documents, cutting the legalisation route for Dutch, Moroccan and other foreign paperwork.

Ongoing

Digital government

Company registration, visas, licensing and compliance run increasingly through online platforms.

Programs and incentives

Programs and incentives worth knowing

01

Regional Headquarters (RHQ) Program

Multinationals that run their MENA operations from Saudi Arabia can access long-term incentives (reported up to 30 years), visa facilitation, premium residency for senior executives and stronger access to government contracts. Real substance is required: an RHQ must genuinely manage the region from the Kingdom.

02

Special Economic Zones (SEZs)

Launched in 2023 to attract industry, logistics and technology, with competitive tax and customs regimes and relaxed rules. Zones include Jazan, Ras Al Khair, King Abdullah Economic City and the Cloud Computing SEZ in Riyadh.

03

Premium Residency

Long-term residency options for investors, executives and skilled professionals, without a local sponsor.

04

Funding and support

National development funds, SME support through Monsha'at, industrial financing and local content programs that reward companies building in the Kingdom.

What this means for you

The door is open. The path is clearer than ever. But clear is not the same as simple. Sector licences, Saudization quotas, local partners and cultural nuance still decide who wins.
